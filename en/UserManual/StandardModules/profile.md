### 1. Feature Introduction
In the Personal Center, the personal information of the currently logged-in user will be displayed. Users can comprehensively view and edit their account information, including name, phone number, email, etc., to ensure the accuracy of the account information. You can enter the Personal Center through the APP Finder or the user dropdown menu.

:::info Note

The personal information edited here will be synchronized with the corresponding user information in the Management Center - Users.

:::

:::warning Tip

The system gives priority to displaying the user's nickname. If the user has not set a nickname, the name will be displayed by default.

:::

### 2. Operation Method
Edit your personal information in the Personal Center. After editing, click "Save" to complete the modification.
