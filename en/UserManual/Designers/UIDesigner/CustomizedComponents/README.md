# Component-based Design
&emsp;&emsp;One of the cores of Oinone's interface designer is its component library, which includes a rich variety of preset components, ranging from basic texts and buttons to complex charts and form elements. They can be added to the canvas through simple drag-and-drop operations to form a complete page layout. This component-based design approach greatly simplifies the development process, improves design efficiency, and ensures design consistency and maintainability.
# Customization Capabilities
&emsp;&emsp;Considering the uniqueness of different projects, Oinone specially supports the function of custom components. When standard components are insufficient to meet specific requirements, users can create their own components to ensure the uniqueness and flexibility of the design. This feature is particularly valuable for highly customized applications, enabling designers to break through limitations and achieve more personalized user experiences.
&emsp;&emsp;Once a component is placed on the canvas, users can deeply adjust its properties. Each component contains multiple elements internally, which are responsible for handling different business logics or displaying specific types of data. Users can make detailed settings for these elements through the property panel, such as adjusting styles and setting interactive behaviors, to ensure that the final design meets actual business needs and achieves the best results.
# Improve Design Efficiency
&emsp;&emsp;By providing an intuitive operation interface and a rich component library, Oinone can significantly shorten the time cycle from concept to finished product and improve overall design efficiency. Both novice and experienced designers can quickly get started and easily complete high-quality design work.
