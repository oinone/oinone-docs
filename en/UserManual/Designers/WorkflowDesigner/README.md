&emsp;&emsp;Oinone's Workflow Designer, as a core module of the Oinone low-code platform, is dedicated to helping users efficiently build and manage various business workflows in an intuitive and visual way. In daily work, we often encounter a variety of workflow requirements, such as approval processes, workflow management, project management, order processing, and customer service. The Workflow Designer can help enterprises digitize these workflows, standardize workflow operations, and ensure operation traceability, thereby significantly improving work efficiency and security.

&emsp;&emsp;The Workflow Designer mainly consists of two major parts: workflow management and workflow design.

+ In the workflow management phase, two display modes are provided: tiled mode and list mode, to meet users' different view preferences and management needs. At the same time, the workflow management function also supports a series of convenient operations such as adding, editing, and deleting workflows, meeting users' flexible and diverse workflow management needs.

+ In the workflow design phase, an intuitive drag-and-drop interface is provided. Users can place workflow nodes on the canvas through simple drag-and-drop operations, and can also set attributes for the nodes, thereby precisely controlling the execution logic of the workflow and quickly building business flowcharts.
