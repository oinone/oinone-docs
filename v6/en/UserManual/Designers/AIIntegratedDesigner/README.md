&emsp;&emsp;Oinone provides a one-stop AI large model integration and design platform that comprehensively covers multiple dimensions of AI technology fields such as voice processing, image generation, text creation, and video synthesis. It supports the rapid access to leading domestic and international large models such as OpenAI, DeepSeek, Tongyi Qianwen, and Stability, and also supports the flexible deployment of various open-source models, enabling enterprises to build an intelligent technology foundation integrating multiple technologies.

&emsp;&emsp;In terms of interaction design, Oinone adopts a visual operation interface, transforming the complex large model integration project into intuitive operations of "dragging nodes + configuring parameters". Enterprise users can complete core processes such as model access, parameter tuning, and data docking through simple interface interactions without relying on professional code development. The platform has a built-in verification function, supporting instant invocation tests and presenting the response results in real-time through a visual dashboard.

&emsp;&emsp;To meet the dynamically evolving business needs of enterprises, Oinone supports the free combination of specialized capability modules such as text generation, image recognition, and data analysis according to specific scenarios such as marketing growth, intelligent customer service, and production optimization. Through the componentized architecture, enterprises can quickly build customized AI solutions, ensuring an accurate match between technological capabilities and business requirements and providing impetus for enterprise intelligentization.
